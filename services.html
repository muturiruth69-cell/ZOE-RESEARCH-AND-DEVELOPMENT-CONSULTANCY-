What We Do

Five service lines. One standard of evidence.

Each line solves a different kind of problem. All of them rest on the same foundation: careful research, human judgement, and a named person accountable for the result.

ResearchIntelligenceAI LabInnovationAcademy
01 · ZOE Research

Evidence you can act on, and defend.

Every organisation eventually faces a decision it cannot make from opinion: enter this market or not, does the programme work, is this feasible. You need evidence with a name on it.

  • Typical timeline4 to 12 weeks
  • MethodMixed-method by default
  • Key deliverableDecision Briefing
  • ForNGOs · corporates · government · universities
Scope a Study

What we deliver

Market & customer studies

Market sizing, segmentation, consumer research, demand assessment, pricing research, market-entry studies.

Programme & evaluation studies

Needs assessments, baselines, midlines, endlines, programme evaluations and outcome studies.

Feasibility & due diligence

Feasibility studies, business-case research and investment due diligence.

Social & policy research

Community research, policy reviews, stakeholder studies and evidence syntheses.

Organisational research

Employee studies, organisational assessments and service-delivery research.

Evidence Assistant Option

Your study’s data, documents and report loaded into a private AI assistant you can query for a year afterwards.

How we work

A quantitative spine (surveys, administrative and secondary data) plus a qualitative layer (key informant interviews, focus groups, observation) that explains why the numbers look the way they do. Fieldwork runs through our certified enumerator network. The analysis plan is written before any data is collected.

Where AI helps, and where it never goes. We use AI for transcription, coding assistance, literature scanning and drafting. Never for sampling, fieldwork, interpretation or sign-off.

What you receive

Research protocolInstrumentsCleaned datasetAnalysisFindings reportDecision briefing

The decision briefing is five to ten pages: what we found, what it means, what we recommend, what we are uncertain about, and what to do on Monday.

04 · ZOE Innovation

Not spending money on the wrong thing.

The most expensive mistake an organisation makes is building something well that nobody wanted. We validate the problem before anyone writes a line of code.

  • Typical timeline6 to 12 weeks per stage
  • MethodHuman-centred design + research spine
  • GatesGo · Iterate · Stop
  • ForStartups · corporates · innovation funders
Validate an Idea

Engagements, stage by stage

Product Validation Sprint

Is this a real problem, for whom, how big, and would they pay?

User research package

Observation, interviews, focus groups, personas and journey maps.

Concept & solution design

Co-design, concepts, business model and prioritisation.

Prototype & testing

Build the cheapest workable version, test it with real users, iterate.

Market entry research

Country, sector, customers, competitors, regulation, pricing, distribution and entry strategy.

Full innovation programme

From problem to validated product to launch plan, end to end.

Our rule: we never prototype before the problem is validated. The whole point of this service is to stop people building the wrong thing, including us.

Every ZOE research proposal can include a priced, optional Phase 2 (prototype and test), so evidence can move straight into a solution when the findings support it.
