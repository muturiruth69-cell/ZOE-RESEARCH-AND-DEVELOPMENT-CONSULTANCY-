Responsible AI: Our Governing Principle

AI assists ZOE researchers. It does not replace them, and it does not decide anything.

ZOE uses artificial intelligence tools to support transcription, document search, data processing and drafting, under a written Responsible AI Policy. All AI-assisted outputs are reviewed and validated by a named ZOE researcher before delivery. AI is not used to generate findings, conclusions or recommendations.

Permitted

With human validation

  • Transcription, with at least 15% of each transcript spot-checked against the audio
  • Translation first-pass, verified by a fluent speaker
  • Literature search, with every cited source opened and verified
  • First-pass coding suggestions, each checked against the actual text
  • Drafting support: raw material, always rewritten by the researcher
  • Tables and charts, with every number verified against the dataset

Restricted

Case-by-case approval

  • Client-confidential information: only in a contracted enterprise instance with no training on inputs, and with your written consent
  • Personal data: only anonymised, or in an assessed, contracted instance
  • AI-assisted classification presented as a finding: method disclosed and validated on a human-coded subsample of at least 10%
  • Automated dashboards and briefings: reviewed by a named researcher on a stated cycle

Prohibited

No exceptions

  • Generating findings, conclusions or recommendations
  • Generating, imputing or completing research data
  • Generating quotes attributed to participants
  • Inventing or “improving” citations: every citation is opened and verified
  • Putting identifiable participant data into any AI tool
  • Automated decisions about individuals without human review

Where a client’s contract prohibits AI use, we comply fully. A copy of our Responsible AI Policy is available on request.

Data Protection

Your data stays yours.

We handle personal data in line with Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019. Client documents, datasets and outputs belong to the client and are never used to train any external AI model.

  • Data-processing agreements signed before any client data is handled
  • Lawful basis and informed consent confirmed for every dataset involving people
  • Participant data de-identified before analysis wherever possible
  • Access limited to the named project team, with permission models on every AI system
  • Client-confidential material never placed in public AI tools
  • Retention and deletion schedules agreed per project

Research Ethics & Quality

Standards that protect participants and findings.

Good research treats respondents with respect and treats evidence honestly.

Informed consent

Every participant knows who we are, why we are asking, how their information will be used, and that they may stop at any time.

Ethics clearance

Where a study requires ethical or regulatory approval, we obtain it before fieldwork, not after.

Certified enumerators

Field teams are trained and certified in interviewing, consent and data quality before they meet a respondent.

Pre-registered analysis

The analysis plan is written before data collection, so findings are discovered, not confirmed.

Independent review

Red Team Read, Associate Review and Client Verification on every study.

Honest limitations

We state plainly what the sample and method can and cannot support.

We decline research designed to justify a decision already taken, and we report findings a client would rather not hear.ZOE research commitment

Academic Integrity

We teach and we guide. We never write examinable work.

ZOE provides training, guidance, methodological consultation and technical services to students and institutions. We do not write theses, dissertations or any examinable work, and we never permit our work to be presented as a student’s own authorship.

This includes the use of AI: we will not use AI to produce examinable academic work for anyone.
