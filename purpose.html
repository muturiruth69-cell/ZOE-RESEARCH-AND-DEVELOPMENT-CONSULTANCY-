What We Work Towards

Vision and mission

Vision

Stronger organisations and communities

To build stronger organisations and communities by transforming research into practical solutions that improve decisions, inspire innovation, and create measurable impact.

Mission

Confident, evidence-based decisions

Our mission is to deliver high-quality research, strategic advisory, professional documentation, and technology-driven solutions that enable our clients to solve complex challenges, seize new opportunities, and make confident, evidence-based decisions. We are committed to excellence, integrity, innovation, and creating measurable impact in every project we undertake.

Our Burden

Our burden is to see…

  1. decisions that shape lives made on evidence, not guesswork;
  2. research leave the shelf and become solutions people actually use;
  3. African organisations own their data, their knowledge and their future;
  4. communities heard, with their realities shaping the programmes meant for them;
  5. technology serve people, with a human always accountable.

Our Promise

Turning Problems into Opportunities

Behind every problem is a decision waiting to be made well. We find the evidence that makes that decision clear, and we stay with you until it becomes a real opportunity.

Our Values

The principles behind every piece of work

Excellence

An analysis plan written before data collection, and methods that stand up to any reviewer.

Integrity

We decline research meant to justify a decision already taken, and we report findings clients may not like.

Innovation

We adopt new tools early and use them to make research faster and better, never less honest.

Measurable Impact

We judge a study by the decision it informed and an AI system by how well it is used after 90 days.

Our Commitments

What every client can expect from us

These commitments apply to every engagement, large or small.

01

A decision at the centre

Every engagement is scoped around the decision it serves, agreed in writing at the start.

02

A named, accountable lead

A real person signs your deliverable and answers for it.

03

Honest findings

Including the ones you would prefer not to hear, with limitations stated plainly.

04

Your data protected

Handled under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019, and never used to train outside AI models.

05

Open about AI use

We tell you where AI assisted, and a researcher checks every output.

06

A clear next step

Every deliverable ends with what to do next, not just what we found.

Our Governing Principle on AI

AI assists ZOE researchers. It does not replace them, and it does not decide anything.

AI is never used to generate findings, data, quotes or citations. Read our full quality and ethics commitments.
