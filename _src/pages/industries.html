The Market We Work In

Why evidence matters more than ever

Organisations across Kenya and East Africa are under growing pressure to show results, use data responsibly and adopt new technology wisely. Each of these pressures is also an opportunity for those who decide well.

  • Accountability for results. Funders, boards and citizens want proof that programmes and investments work.
  • Competitive markets. Businesses entering or expanding need reliable insight on customers, pricing and competitors.
  • The arrival of AI. Many organisations want AI but are unsure where it helps, what it costs and what the risks are.
  • Data protection. Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019 raises the standard for how personal data is collected and used.
  • Knowledge locked in documents. Years of reports and manuals sit unused because nobody can search them.

Policy grounded in evidence
