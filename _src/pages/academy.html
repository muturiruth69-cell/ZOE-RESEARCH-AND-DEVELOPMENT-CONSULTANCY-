Our Integrity Boundary

We teach and we guide. We never write examinable work.

ZOE provides training, guidance, methodological consultation and technical services. We do not write theses, dissertations or any examinable work, and we never permit our work to be presented as a student’s own authorship.

This rule is non-negotiable. It protects students, institutions and the credibility of research itself.
