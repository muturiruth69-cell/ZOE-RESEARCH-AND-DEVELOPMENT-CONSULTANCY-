Our Story

Why ZOE exists

Across Africa, important decisions are made every day in boardrooms, county offices, donor programmes and community halls. Too many of them are made on assumption rather than evidence. Good research existed, but it often arrived late, sat on a shelf, or answered the wrong question.

ZOE was founded to build a different kind of research firm: one that begins with the decision, not the questionnaire; one that goes to the field and listens before it concludes; and one that is honest about what the evidence can and cannot support.

As technology changed, so did we. Modern tools now handle routine tasks such as transcription and first drafts, which frees our people to focus on what matters most: asking the right question, reaching the right people, checking that the data is real, interpreting it in context, and standing behind the result.

Today ZOE helps clients move from question, to evidence, to a working solution, with one partner accountable for the whole journey. That is what we mean by turning problems into opportunities.
