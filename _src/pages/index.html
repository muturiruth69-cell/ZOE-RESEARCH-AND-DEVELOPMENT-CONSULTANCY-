Who We Are

A research and development firm that turns questions into answers you can use

ZOE Research & Development Consultancy works with governments, NGOs, businesses, investors and institutions across Kenya and East Africa. When an organisation faces a difficult question, such as whether to enter a new market, whether a programme is really working, or where technology could help, we find out what is true and help them act on it.

On any given day our team is framing a client’s question, training field researchers, interviewing customers and community members, cleaning and analysing data, building AI tools on a client’s own documents, or teaching professionals to use evidence well. Every piece of work ends with a clear recommendation and a named person who is accountable for it.

How a Project Works at ZOE

Four steps from your problem to your opportunity

Whether the work is a ten-day market sprint or a year-long programme evaluation, every ZOE engagement follows the same disciplined path.

Listen and frame

We meet you, understand the decision you face, and agree in writing exactly what the work must answer.

Gather evidence

Surveys, interviews, focus groups and data reviews, run by trained researchers who respect every respondent.

Analyse and advise

We analyse the data, test our findings with you, and present clear and practical recommendations.

Build and follow through

Where it helps, we prototype solutions, build AI tools, train your team and review results after 90 days.
