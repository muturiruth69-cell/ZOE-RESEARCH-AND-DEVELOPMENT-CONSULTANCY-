Questions

Frequently asked

Still unsure? Call or WhatsApp us and we will talk it through.

How much does a study cost?
It depends on the question, the population and the method. We scope every engagement around the decision it serves and quote a fixed price for a fixed scope. Our three starter offers are designed to be small enough to approve quickly.
How quickly can you start?
A Rapid Market Sprint can begin within days of agreement and is delivered in ten working days. Larger field studies depend on ethics clearance, sampling and timing, and we give you an honest timeline up front.
Do you use AI in your research?
Yes, openly and under a written Responsible AI Policy. We use AI for transcription, search, data processing and drafting support. Every AI-assisted output is checked by a named researcher, and AI never generates findings, data, quotes or citations. If your contract does not allow AI use, we comply fully.
Will my information be kept confidential?
Yes. We handle data under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019, sign data-processing agreements before handling client data, and never use your content to train any outside AI model.
Where do you work?
Our office is at Wuyi Plaza, Westlands, Nairobi. We work across Kenya and the wider East African region, with fieldwork delivered through our trained field network.
Can you help with my thesis?
We offer training, methodology consultation and technical support such as instrument design and analysis guidance. We do not write theses, dissertations or any examinable work.
